What should I write in my first message?

Your business, what you want to make or fix, and your deadline if you have one. Links to sites you like help, but you don't need them.

Do I need to pay for a domain and hosting?

A domain (like yourbusiness.com) is usually paid yearly. Hosting for a simple site can be low-cost or free. I'll list every cost before we start, so there are no surprises.

What if I need changes after it goes live?

Message me. Small changes like text or photos are quick. For bigger changes I'll tell you the price first.

Can I see an example first?

Yes. Open the live EOD tool and the sample clinic site. I can also show the app's login side on a call.
